PRIVACY POLICY

Electro Mechanical Services (EMS)

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to EMS Store App

📋 1. Overview

EMS Store is an offline-first Point of Sale and Inventory Management application built exclusively for Electro Mechanical Services. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your data.

✅ We do NOT collect, sell, or share any personal data with third parties.

💾 2. Data Storage

All business data — including products, sales records, customer accounts, and store settings — is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB (browser storage). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable Google Drive sync.

☁️ 3. Google Drive Integration

Google Drive sync is an optional feature. When enabled:

• We request access only to files created by this app (using the drive.file scope).

• Your database is backed up to your personal Google Drive under the folder "ElectroMechanicalServices_DB".

• We do not access any other files in your Google Drive.

• You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings.

🔑 4. Google OAuth & Permissions

When connecting Google Drive, we use Google's secure OAuth 2.0 protocol. We only request the minimum necessary permissions:

🔹 drive.file — Read and write only the EMS backup file we create
🔹 userinfo.email — Display your connected email address

🚫 5. What We Do NOT Do

• We do not operate any servers or external databases.

• We do not collect analytics, usage data, or crash reports.

• We do not display advertisements.

• We do not share your data with any third parties.

🔐 6. Security

Access to the EMS Store app is protected by PIN/Password authentication. Only authorized personnel with valid credentials can access store data. We recommend keeping your PIN confidential and changing it regularly from Settings.
